# Letter of Intent — Skeleton

One page. Adapt the bracketed parts. Delete this header before sending.

---

[Your Name], PharmD Candidate 20XX
[School of Pharmacy]
[Email] · [Phone]

[Date]

[Preceptor / Program Coordinator Name, credentials]
[Title]
[Organization]
[Address]

Dear [Name / "Members of the Selection Committee"],

**¶1 — Hook and thesis.**
I am writing to apply for the [exact program name] for the [dates / academic year] rotation
cycle. I am a [P3/P4] student at [school], and I learned about the program through
[specific source — a preceptor, IPhO, your student chapter, the organization's site].
[One sentence naming what specifically draws you to *this* organization — a therapeutic area,
a policy the organization drove, a function you want to learn. If this sentence would survive
swapping in a different organization's name, rewrite it.]

**¶2 — Your strongest evidence.**
[Pick ONE experience and go deep. A patient interaction that changed your thinking. A
recommendation you made and what happened. A project and its outcome. Say what you did, what
resulted, and what you took from it. Resist the urge to list — a list makes this a prose CV
and reviewers stop reading.]

**¶3 — The bridge to this program.**
[Connect that experience to the specific work of this organization. Name a function:
regulatory affairs, medical information, formulary strategy, health policy — not "industry"
or "managed care" in the abstract. Show you know what the rotation actually involves.]

**¶4 — Close.**
[What you expect to gain, and what you'd contribute — a skill, a work ethic, a specific
capability. State your available rotation blocks. Confirm your school's experiential office
is prepared to coordinate the affiliation agreement.] Thank you for your consideration; I
would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application further.

Sincerely,
[Your Name], PharmD Candidate 20XX

---

## Checklist before sending

- [ ] One page
- [ ] Organization named at least twice, with something specific to it
- [ ] A *function* named, not just a field
- [ ] One deep example, not a list
- [ ] No typos in the organization's name or the recipient's credentials
- [ ] Read out loud once
- [ ] Read by one other person
- [ ] Saved as PDF: `Lastname_Firstname_LOI_[Organization].pdf`
- [ ] Any file-naming convention the program specified is followed exactly
