## Named industry APPE programs

### Bristol Myers Squibb — APPE Program 🔴 open now
**Location:** Princeton / Lawrenceville, NJ; New York, NY
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks
**Reported window:** **September 1 – September 19**. Late materials are not accepted.
**Link:** [careers.bms.com/advanced-degree-programs](https://careers.bms.com/advanced-degree-programs/)

**Functional areas offered:** medical affairs, clinical development, health economics &
outcomes research (HEOR), field medical, marketing, strategy, and policy. That breadth is
unusual — most industry APPEs are single-function.

**Application materials:** a **one-page letter of intent** focused on how a BMS rotation
advances your specific career goals, plus enrollment in an accredited pharmacy program with
intent to complete rotations in the following year.

> This is the highest-value deadline on the calendar as of today. If industry is your
> direction, treat it as this week's priority.

### Boehringer Ingelheim — PharmD Advanced Pharmacy Practice Experiences Program
**Location:** Ridgefield, CT (US headquarters)

## Contract research and clinical trials

Under-applied-to and often more willing to host students than big pharma.

| Organization | Location(s) | Angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IQVIA** | Durham NC, and offices nationwide | [Medical & scientific writing](https://www.iqvia.com/library/fact-sheets/medical-and-scientific-writing-solutions), real-world evidence, HEOR |
| **Parexel** | Newton MA, Durham NC | [Medical writing careers](https://jobs.parexel.com/en/medicalwritingjobs); emerging talent programs |
| **Syneos Health / Inizio** | Morrisville NC, NJ | Medical affairs, commercial |
| **Fortrea, ICON, PPD/Thermo Fisher** | Various | Clinical operations, pharmacovigilance |
| **Academic trial units** | Any AMC near you | Investigational Drug Service — see below |

**Investigational Drug Service (IDS)** rotations at academic medical centers are the most
accessible on-ramp to clinical research and often the easiest to arrange, since they're
in-house at hospitals your school already contracts with. ASHP publishes a
[sample IDS rotation description](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/pharmacy-practice/resource-centers/preceptor-toolkit/sicp-investigational-drug-appe-student-rotation-updated.pdf)
you can hand a preceptor who's never hosted a student before.

## What the functional areas actually are

Useful for writing a letter of intent that doesn't sound generic.

| Function | What a pharmacist does there |
| --- | --- |
| **Medical Affairs** | Scientific exchange with clinicians; the bridge between R&D and practice |
| **Medical Information** | Answers unsolicited clinical questions; writes standard response documents |
| **MSL (field medical)** | Field-based scientific liaison to KOLs; heavy travel, high autonomy |
| **Regulatory Affairs** | Submissions (IND, NDA, BLA), labeling, agency interaction |
| **Clinical Development** | Protocol design, trial execution, data interpretation |
| **Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety** | Adverse event assessment, signal detection, REMS |
| **HEOR** | Cost-effectiveness, value dossiers, payer evidence |
| **Medical Writing** | Protocols, clinical study reports, manuscripts, regulatory documents |
| **Commercial / Marketing** | Brand strategy, launch planning, market access |
| **Market Access / Payer** | Formulary strategy, contracting, AMCP dossiers |

## The fellowship pipeline (plan backwards from here)

Industry APPEs and post-grad industry fellowships are the same ecosystem. If a fellowship is
the goal, know the programs now — they shape which APPEs matter.

| Program | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **[Rutgers Institute for Pharmaceutical Industry Fellowships (RPIF)](https://pharmafellows.rutgers.edu/)** | The largest. ~19 partner companies, 160+ fellows/year. [Sponsor companies & brochures](https://pharmafellows.rutgers.edu/sponsor-companies/) — brochures update each August. |
| **UNC Eshelman** | e.g. [BMS clinical pharmacology fellowship](https://pharmacy.unc.edu/education/fellowships/bristol-myers-squibb/) |
| **Purdue** | [FDA and industry-affiliated programs](https://www.phpr.purdue.edu/fellowships/fda-and-industry-affiliated-programs/drug-information) |
| **MCPHS, Northeastern, St. John's, Temple, USciences** | Regional industry fellowship partnerships across the Northeast |

Fellowship recruiting happens at **ASHP Midyear** and the industry-specific showcases. Being
a P4 with an industry APPE already on your CV is a meaningful advantage there.

## Tactics for landing an industry rotation

1. **Deadlines are early and hard.** BMS's window is 19 days long. Missing it costs a year.
2. **Check the school-agreement constraint first.** BI and several others only take students
   from partner schools. This is the most common silent disqualifier.
3. **Your letter of intent must name a function.** "I'm interested in industry" reads as
   unserious. "I want to build toward regulatory affairs, and BMS's global regulatory group
   is where I'd learn submission strategy" reads as a candidate.
4. **Join IPhO and show up.** Chapter leadership and IPhO's industry rounds are the cheapest
   networking available to a student.
5. **A local CRO or an IDS rotation is a real industry credential** if the big names don't
   come through. Don't treat them as consolation prizes — they teach the same vocabulary.
6. **LinkedIn works here** in a way it doesn't for hospital rotations. Alumni from your school
   at target companies are your best route to a preceptor who isn't advertising.
