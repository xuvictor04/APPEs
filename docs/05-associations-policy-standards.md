# 05 — Associations, Policy & Standards APPEs

Back to [index](../README.md).

Almost every national pharmacy organization runs a competitive association-management APPE,
and nearly all of them are clustered in the **DC metro area** — Bethesda, Rockville,
Alexandria, Arlington, and DC proper. If you're Northeast/Mid-Atlantic, this is a short trip
and a dense set of applications you can batch.

These rotations are consistently described as career-changing by students who do them, and
they're strikingly under-applied-to relative to clinical electives.

---

strong CV and one adaptable letter of intent covers most of the set.

## ASHP — Association Management APPE
**Location:** Bethesda, MD
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks
**Link:** [ashp.org/appe](https://www.ashp.org/appe) ·
[Resident rotation](https://www.ashp.org/resident-rotation)

**Deadline: none.** ASHP accepts applications **year-round on an ongoing basis** — this is one
of the few competitive association rotations without a fixed cutoff. That makes it a good
late-cycle option once the block-deadline programs have closed.

**Scheduling constraint:** placements are **not available in December, May, June, or July**.
Plan your block calendar around that before applying.

**Eligibility:** full-time enrollment in an ACPE-accredited PharmD program, good academic
standing, **active ASHP membership**, and demonstrated involvement in professional
organizations.

**Related:** the [ASHP Executive Fellowship in Association Leadership & Management](https://www.ashp.org/about-ashp/ashp-executive-fellowship/application-and-program-details)
— a paid 12-month post-grad program at Bethesda HQ. Worth knowing if this rotation lands well.

**Bonus resource:** ASHP publishes free, well-built **sample rotation descriptions** you can
give to a preceptor who wants to host you but has never built a syllabus:
[medication safety](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/pharmacy-practice/resource-centers/preceptor-toolkit/sicp-med-safety-appe-student-rotation-updated.pdf) ·
[informatics](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/pharmacy-practice/resource-centers/preceptor-toolkit/sicp-informatics-appe-student-rotation-updated.pdf) ·
[emergency medicine](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/pharmacy-practice/resource-centers/preceptor-toolkit/sicp-emergency-appe-student-rotation-updated.pdf) ·
[investigational drug service](https://www.ashp.org/-/media/assets/pharmacy-practice/resource-centers/preceptor-toolkit/sicp-investigational-drug-appe-student-rotation-updated.pdf).
This is a genuinely useful lever for creating a rotation that doesn't exist yet.

## APhA — Experiential Program in Association Management & Leadership
**Location:** Washington, DC (APhA headquarters)
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks, final-year elective
**Applications:** accepted **year-round**; decisions typically within about a month
**Link:** [APhA Experiential Programs](https://www.pharmacist.com/Membership/Student-Pharmacists/APhA-Experiential-Programs) ·
[Application form](https://fs8.formsite.com/APhA-ASP/appeapp/index.html)

Broadest scope of the association rotations — APhA covers the whole profession rather than
one practice setting. Good fit if your interest is advocacy, professional policy, or
student/new-practitioner programming. Natural for APhA-ASP chapter officers.

---

## AMCP — Experiential Program in Association & Leadership Management
**Location:** Alexandria, VA
**Link:** [Program page](https://www.amcp.org/resource-center/group-resources/residents-fellows/residencies-fellowships/amcp-experiential-program-association-leadership-management) ·
[AMCP APPE rotation](https://www.amcp.org/resource-center/group-resources/student-pharmacist-center/APPE-IPPE-Directory/amcp-appe-rotation)

**Duration:** 4–5 weeks with a **flexible end date** designed to fit most APPE schedules.

**Application materials:**
- Completed APPE application
- **500-word essay** on career goals and interest in managed care and/or association management
- Current CV/resume with references
- Full-time enrollment in an ACPE-accredited program, with the graduation year specified for
  the cycle you're applying to

**Don't miss the directory:** AMCP maintains an
[**APPE/IPPE Directory**](https://www.amcp.org/resource-center/group-resources/student-pharmacist-center/APPE-IPPE-Directory/amcp-appe-rotation)
and a [managed care internships page](https://www.amcp.org/resource-center/group-resources/student-pharmacist-center/managed-care-internships)
listing rotations at **health plans and PBMs across the country** — far more sites than AMCP's
own headquarters rotation. This is the highest-yield single link for managed care. See
[06 — Managed Care & PBM](06-managed-care-pbm.md).

Also useful: the [AMCP APPE curriculum and program description (PDF)](https://www.amcp.org/sites/default/files/2019-11/APPE_Curriculum.pdf).

AMCP also publishes [How to Develop a Managed Care Pharmacy IPPE/APPE](https://www.amcp.org/resource/how-develop-managed-care-pharmacy-ippeappe)
— hand this to a health plan pharmacist who's willing but doesn't know how to structure it.

## ACCP — Professional Leadership APPE 🔴
**Reported window:** applications accepted **August 1 – September 15** each year
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks
**Link:** [Program page](https://www.accp.com/education/appe.aspx) ·
[Syllabus & objectives](https://www.accp.com/education/appe_syll_obj.aspx) ·
[Student resources](https://www.accp.com/stunet/)

**Application requirements — note the lead time on these:**
- Completed application and CV
- **Two letters of recommendation from faculty members**
- Documented active participation in student pharmacy organizations
- **A letter from your experiential education program administrator** confirming scheduling
  and that an affiliation agreement can be established

That last item means you must talk to your OEE *before* submitting, and the two faculty
letters mean you need to ask this week if you're applying this cycle.

**Content:** leadership inside a professional organization seen from both the elected-leader
and staff sides — discussions, journal clubs, presentations, and writing projects.

## AACP — Association Management APPE
**Location:** Arlington, VA
**Reported deadline:** materials to **APPE@aacp.org by November 1**
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks. **Available months are generally September, October, January, March,
and April** — check that against your block calendar before you apply.
**Link:** [Student Pharmacist APPE Application](https://www.aacp.org/form/student-pharmacist-appe-application) ·
[Syllabus (PDF)](https://www.aacp.org/sites/default/files/2022-08/aacp-appe-syllabus.pdf)

You would work closely with association leaders including senior directors and vice presidents.
Selected students need a **strong interest in national association management and academia**.
This is the single best association rotation if your goal is a career in pharmacy education.

**Pair it with:** [AACP Aspiring Academics Program](https://www.aacp.org/resource/aacp-aspiring-academics-program)
and AACP's programming on [clinical academia APPE rotations](https://www.aacp.org/event/clinic-classroom-perspectives-clinical-academia-appe-rotations).
See [07 — Academia, Teaching & Research](07-academia-teaching-research.md).

## NACDS — Association Management APPE
**Location:** Arlington, VA
**Reported deadline:** applications by **January 16** receive priority review
**Format:** onsite Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday, remote option Monday and Friday — a hybrid
schedule that makes commuting from the Northeast corridor plausible
**Link:** [nacds.org/appe](https://www.nacds.org/appe/)

**Eligibility — note the timing requirement:** full-time enrollment in an accredited U.S.
school or college of pharmacy, good academic standing, and **in your final didactic year**
(i.e., you apply as a P3). **Chain community pharmacy experience is preferred** — if you have
retail intern hours, say so explicitly in your application.

Focus is the chain community pharmacy sector: federal and state policy affecting retail
pharmacy, reimbursement, scope of practice, and the business of community pharmacy. Strong
complement to a community core rotation if you want to understand *why* retail practice
looks the way it does.

## NCPA — Elective Rotation in Association Management
**Location:** Alexandria, VA (at the King Street Metro — easy transit access)
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks, flexible scheduling
**Applications:** **year-round**; notification typically within a month
**Link:** [ncpa.org](https://ncpa.org/) ·
[FAQs (PDF)](https://ncpa.org/sites/default/files/2020-08/faqs-ncpa-experiential-rotation.pdf) ·
[Student programs](https://ncpa.org/newsroom/qam/2024/11/20/students-dont-miss-out-ncpas-career-advancing-programs)

Independent community pharmacy ownership, advocacy, and the economics of independents.
The rolling deadline makes this a good late-cycle option when block-deadline programs have
closed.

## ASCP — Association Leadership & Management APPE
**Location:** Alexandria, VA
**Link:** [ascp.com/page/APPE](https://www.ascp.com/page/APPE) — the page is currently titled
**"APPE Rotation for 2027–2028 Academic Year,"** so this cycle is live. Also see the
[senior care student rotation guidelines (PDF)](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.ascp.com/resource/resmgr/docs/membership/students-rotations-guideline.pdf)
and [ASCP student page](https://www.ascp.com/page/students).

Non-patient-care elective in senior care pharmacy and consultant pharmacy, with a strong
continuing-education and professional-society component. Relevant if you're interested in
geriatrics, long-term care, or deprescribing.

## PQA — Pharmacy Quality Alliance APPE ✅ status corrected

**Location:** **remote, or at PQA headquarters** in the Washington, DC metro area
**Timing (verified July 2026):** applications **open in September**, are **due in November**,
and offers go out in **December**
**Email:** Academics@PQAalliance.org
**Link:** [pqaalliance.org/appe](https://www.pqaalliance.org/appe) ·
[Student programs](https://www.pqaalliance.org/student-programs) ·
[2026–2027 Student and Professional Programs Guide](https://www.pqaalliance.org/assets/PQA_Student_and_Fellowship_Programs.pdf)

> ⚠️ **Correction.** An earlier draft of these docs listed **December 31**, taken from an older
> cycle page. The verified timing is **due in November**, with offers in December. Applications
> are open right now.

**Materials — note the two easy-to-miss items:**
- One-page cover letter
- CV
- **Unofficial transcript**
- One recommendation
- **A letter of approval from your school's experiential program**

That last one means an OEE conversation has to happen before you submit, not after.

**Content:** a final-year elective in research and performance measurement, medication-use
quality education, nonprofit/association management, and member communications. PQA develops
the quality measures behind Medicare Part D Star Ratings — if you want to understand how
pharmacy quality is actually scored nationally, this is the source.

**The remote option matters:** you can do this rotation without relocating to DC.

## USP — United States Pharmacopeia
**Location:** Rockville, MD
**Format:** a select number of **non-traditional APPE rotations** for PharmD candidates,
offered throughout the year
**Link:** [USP Academic Connection](https://www.usp.org/services/academic-connection)

You learn how USP creates healthcare standards nationally and internationally, and how those
standards protect the quality of medicines, excipients, biologics, dietary supplements, food
ingredients, and global health supply. If you've ever cited USP <795>, <797>, or <800> in a
class, this is where those come from.

Contact through the Academic Connection page; specific application mechanics aren't posted
publicly, so treat this as a structured cold-outreach target.

## NABP and state boards of pharmacy
**NABP:** Mount Prospect, IL — [nabp.pharmacy](https://nabp.pharmacy/) ·
[Regulatory and policy resources](https://nabp.pharmacy/members/programs-services/regulatory-and-policy-resources/)

NABP doesn't advertise a standing APPE program, but it monitors federal legislation and
regulation through its Federal Affairs department and accredits programs (DMEPOS, .pharmacy
domains, drug distributor accreditation). Worth an inquiry if regulation is your interest.

**More accessible: your state board of pharmacy.** Several boards host students for
inspection ride-alongs, disciplinary case review, and rulemaking. NABP's member directory
lists every board. In the Northeast, look at the boards of pharmacy in NJ, NY, PA, MD, CT,
and MA. These are almost always cold-outreach arrangements, and they're rarely competitive
because so few students think to ask.

## State and regional pharmacy associations — an underused category

National associations get all the attention, but **state associations run APPE rotations too**,
and far fewer students apply. Two with documented, publicly posted programs:

- **[North Carolina Association of Pharmacists — APPE Elective Rotations](https://www.ncpharmacists.org/appe-elective-rotations)**
- **[California Pharmacists Association — APPE Program](https://cpha.com/advanced-pharmacy-practice-experience-program/)** —
  posts specific rotation blocks with their own deadlines (e.g., a February block with a
  preceding November deadline)

**In your region**, these have active student divisions and are worth a direct inquiry even
where no APPE is advertised — a state association is small enough that one email can create a
rotation that doesn't exist yet:

| Association | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [Pennsylvania Pharmacists Association](https://www.papharmacists.com/students/) | Active student programming |
| [Pharmacists Society of the State of New York (PSSNY)](https://www.pssny.org/) | Has a student division (SPSSNY) |
| [New York State Council of Health-system Pharmacists](https://www.nyschp.org/) | Health-system focus |
| [NYC Society of Health-system Pharmacists](https://nycshp.org/) | Metro-area network |
| New Jersey Pharmacists Association | Advocacy and licensure policy |
| Maryland Pharmacists Association · Massachusetts Pharmacists Association · Connecticut Pharmacists Association | Regional advocacy |

**Full directory:** [State pharmacy associations list](https://www.papharmacists.com/page/StateAssociations) —
every state has one.

**Why bother:** state-level advocacy is where scope-of-practice and reimbursement law actually
gets made, and a state association rotation puts you in the room for it. The ask is also far
lower-stakes than a national program — you're often talking to a small staff who would welcome
the help.

**Related credentialing body:** the [Board of Pharmacy Specialties](https://bpsweb.org/) has
hosted APPE students and published a
[student's account of the rotation](https://bpsweb.org/2024/08/29/on-rotation-an-appe-students-perspective-of-pharmacist-certification/).

## Batch-applying: the efficient play

These programs want nearly identical materials. Build once, adapt many:

1. **One master CV** (see [11](11-application-materials.md)).
2. **One letter-of-intent skeleton**, with a swappable middle section naming the specific
   organization's work.
3. **Two faculty letters of recommendation** — ask once, tell your letter writers you're
   applying to several, and give them the full list plus deadlines in one email.
4. **One OEE conversation** covering all of them — ask which already have agreements with
   your school. Several will.
5. **Membership**: join ASHP, APhA, AMCP, and ACCP as a student. Dues are low, several
   require active membership to apply, and involvement is explicitly scored.

Realistic expectation: these cohorts are small — often a handful of students per year per
organization. Apply to six, expect one or two.
