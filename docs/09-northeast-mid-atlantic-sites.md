# 09 — Northeast & Mid-Atlantic Site Directory

Back to [index](../README.md).

> **How to read this file.** This is a **target list**, not a list of confirmed APPE sites.
> These are major teaching hospitals, health systems, and employers in the region — nearly all
> have pharmacy departments and most have residency programs, which is the strongest signal
> that they precept students. **Whether *your school* can place you there depends entirely on
> the affiliation agreement.** Take this list to your OEE and ask which ones are already
> covered. That single conversation will cut it down fast.

Sites with a nationally-advertised application are marked 🌐 and linked.

## How to work this list

1. **Copy 15–20 names** into your [tracker](../templates/application-tracker.csv).
2. **Take it to your OEE.** Ask which have current affiliation agreements. Expect roughly
   half to already be covered.
3. **For the covered ones**, request them through your school's scheduling system.
4. **For the uncovered ones you really want**, ask how long a new agreement takes — and
   whether the school will start one. Then cold-email a preceptor there. Template in
   [11](11-application-materials.md).
5. **Sites with a 🌐 have their own application** — those are on the calendar in
   [02](02-timeline-and-deadlines.md) and don't wait for your school's process.
