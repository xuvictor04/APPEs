# 06 — Managed Care, PBM & Payer APPEs

Back to [index](../README.md).

Managed care is the largest employer of non-dispensing pharmacists and the least visible to
students. It's also unusually welcoming to APPE students, because much of the work is
project-based and portable.

---

## The one link that matters most

**[AMCP APPE/IPPE Directory + Managed Care Internships](https://www.amcp.org/resource-center/group-resources/student-pharmacist-center/managed-care-internships)**

AMCP maintains a searchable directory of managed care rotations and internships submitted by
health plans, PBMs, and consultancies nationwide. Sites add themselves via AMCP's
[APPE/IPPE submission form](https://www.amcp.org/appeippe-submission-form), so it reflects
who is *currently* looking for students — which no static list, including this one, can.

Check it first, then use the sections below to fill gaps and target specific companies.

## What you'd actually do

The deliverables are the reason this rotation is good for your CV — they're concrete and
you can talk about them in an interview.

- **Formulary review** — write a drug monograph for a P&T committee
- **P&T committee** — attend, and often present
- **Prior authorization criteria** — draft or revise clinical criteria
- **Drug utilization review** — retrospective and concurrent
- **AMCP Format dossier review** — evaluate a manufacturer's value dossier
- **Medicare Part D Star Ratings** — adherence measures, MTM completion rates
- **Specialty pharmacy management** — high-cost drug strategy, biosimilar conversion
- **HEOR / budget impact modeling**
- **Medication therapy management (MTM)** program operations
- **Value-based and outcomes-based contracts**

## Specialty pharmacy and adjacent

A fast-growing sector with real student demand, and often easier to arrange than a PBM.

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Specialty pharmacies** | Accredo (Evernorth), CVS Specialty, Optum Specialty, BioPlus, Onco360 (NJ/NY) |
| **Health-system specialty pharmacies** | Penn Medicine, Mount Sinai, Johns Hopkins, Yale New Haven, Northwell — most large AMCs now run one |
| **Home infusion** | Option Care Health, Soleo Health, health-system infusion services |
| **340B programs** | Hospital 340B compliance offices — an underrated rotation, deeply relevant to policy |
| **GPOs** | Vizient (Irving TX), Premier (Charlotte NC) |
| **Consulting** | Milliman, Avalere (DC), IQVIA Consulting, Mercer, Willis Towers Watson |

**340B is worth singling out.** Every disproportionate-share hospital has a 340B team, the
program is under constant policy pressure, and almost no students ask for the rotation. It
sits at the intersection of finance, compliance, and policy — a rare combination on a P4 CV.

## Building the case in your application

Managed care rotations respond well to specificity. Concrete things you can point to:

- **AMCP student chapter** involvement, especially the **P&T Competition** — this is the
  single most recognized managed care credential a student can have
- Any **drug information** or **formulary/monograph** work from coursework
- Comfort with **claims data, Excel, and basic biostatistics**
- A stated interest in a *function* — "I want to work on specialty drug policy," not
  "I'm interested in managed care"

If your school has an AMCP chapter, the P&T Competition alone is worth the time investment
before you apply.
